Course Overview

Welcome to Geometry at Saint Peter the Rock Academy. This portal presents our complete academic year schedule covering Chapters 5 through 12 of Holt Geometry, along with daily assignments, checkpoint dates, examination schedules, and supplemental video resources.

Assessment & Routine

In-class instruction takes place twice weekly every Tuesday and Thursday, where attendance and active participation during demonstrations are required. Focused daily homework problem sets out of Holt Geometry are scheduled for Wednesday, Friday, Saturday, and Monday. Chapter Checkpoint assignments are completed on Mondays following each chapter's completion.
