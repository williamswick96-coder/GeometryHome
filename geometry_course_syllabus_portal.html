Class Schedule

In-class instruction takes place twice a week every Tuesday and Thursday. Attendance and active participation during lectures are required.

Homework Routine

Short daily assignments (~30 minutes) are assigned for Wednesday, Friday, Saturday, and Monday, out of Holt Geometry.

Assessments

  • Checkpoint Challenge Assignments: Completed at the end of each chapter.
  • Fall Semester: 2 In-Class Tests.
  • Spring Semester: 3 In-Class Tests + Comprehensive Final Exam.

Accessing Your Work

To submit homework grade tokens: Copy and paste the grade token which is available after to complete all problems on that day. The dropdown sub pages located in the upper right contain modules for each non-school day. You may complete them at your own pace but daily work is strongly recomended. All sub pages and new work will be updated each Tuesday evening.
